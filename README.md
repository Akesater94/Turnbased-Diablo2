# Turnbased/Idle diablo 2.
During a course in php I started this as a side project along our school assignments. There was no plan, no clear idea of what I wanted this project to be. It was a playground for me to test out some things i learned during the course and it just grew. Most of the time I was not fully grasping what I was doing. I was just piling it on top of the already existing code. Similiar to how my two year old daugther plays with her wooden blocks. Eventhough the project is small, it is has an incredible technical debt to it. Trying to implement new features is a dauting task to me now since I haven't touched it in almost 6months.

Despite all it's flaws, I had so much fun with this project and I think that's why I wanted to share it here. If I ever have a reason to get into PHP and JS again I will revisit this project to either burn most of it to the ground and start over or to try and restructure what is there.

## Setup
  -Install Laragon from here: https://laragon.org/download  
  -Download the repository and put the "tbd2" folder inside Laragons "www" folder. Example: C:/Laragon/www  
  -Start the laragon application, ignore and close any license key prompts.  
  -Press "Start all" on the bottom left corner of the laragon application.  
  -go to "http://localhost/tbd2/public/" in your web browser and get going :)
  
## How do I play?  
  Make an account and login. Create a character and you will be placed in the town area.  
  Use "I" to open up your inventory window.  
  Use "C" to open up your stat window, here you can increase your stats when you level up.  
  
## While in town  
  There are three icons in the bottom center of your screen.  
  -Use them to either buy and sell gear (no currency is present as of now)  
  -Travel outside of town to battle monsters and level up  
  -Open your inventory and equip weapons and armor. 
  
## When outside town  
  Press space to start fightning monsters  
  Press the potions icon to heal up  
  Press the town portal icon in the bottom left corner to get back to town.

## Features that are missing and aren't "bugs"
  You cannot die in combat.  
  There is no currency, you simply trade items with the shopkeeper.  
  The energy stat doesn't change any of the players stats so it is basically useless (kind of like real d2 if you're not playing a pesky sorc).  
  None of the playable classes have skills.
